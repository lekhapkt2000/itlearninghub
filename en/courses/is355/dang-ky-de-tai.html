Only one member per group (ideally the leader) needs to fill in and submit this form. Before registering, read Project topics and Project levels in the Project Lab, and check the Topic list to avoid overlapping.

Need to change something after submitting? Submit the form again with the same leader student ID and verify the OTP with the email of a member already in the earlier submission - The new version replaces the old one. What you type is saved as a draft in this browser, so reloading the page won't lose it.
01

Group

A group may mix students from IS355.R11.CTTT.1 and IS355.R11.CTTT.2. The system assigns a random group code after you register.

02

Members

Row 1 is the group leader, up to 6 members. Just enter the student ID: The name, class and email (ID@gm.uit.edu.vn) fill in automatically. Empty rows are ignored.

03

Project

Write the title in English, as on the registration template. Write the short description in both Vietnamese and English, 3-5 sentences each: The problem, data, method/model, the Blockchain part (smart contract, testnet) and the demo interface.

04

References

At least 3, at most 5. You need at least 1 Dataset, 1 Method and 1 Related Survey. Give a full citation or a link (DOI, arXiv, Kaggle, GitHub).

05

Self-check before submitting

Updates as you type. Missing items do not block submission, but they are what the instructor usually asks about.

06

Email verification

Whoever submits the form (the leader or a member) picks their own school email, clicks Send OTP and enters the 6-digit code. After registering, the whole group receives a confirmation email.

Your information goes to the course instructor and is only used to manage course projects.
