COURSE INTRODUCTION

IS355 – Blockchain Technology (Công nghệ Blockchain)

Explore the foundations of Blockchain technology, how it works, its ecosystem and real-world applications; while practicing how to analyze finance and banking problems that Blockchain could solve, and develop that analysis into a course project. Unlike IT004 (which focuses on SQL skills), IS355 follows the model Topic → Foundational Knowledge → Practice Exercises → Application Analysis → Idea Formation → Project Development, taking students from "What is Blockchain?" to "How can I research and build a Blockchain application project?"

LEARNING OUTCOMES

After 6 weeks, students will be able to

Foundational knowledge

  • Understand the core concepts and components of Blockchain
  • Distinguish Blockchain from traditional databases
  • Understand transactions, blocks, hashing, digital signatures, and consensus
  • Learn about Smart Contracts and their applications

Application analysis

  • Analyze Blockchain use cases in finance and banking
  • Identify problems well-suited to a Blockchain solution

Project development

  • Form an idea and propose a project
  • Design the solution architecture
  • Carry out research, experiments, or a prototype
  • Present and defend the project results

Course philosophy

IS355 will not turn into a "learn Solidity, then code a Smart Contract" course. The structure follows Business Problem → Blockchain Suitability → Research → Architecture → Prototype → Evaluation, not Blockchain → Solidity → Code → find an application to demo. This lets students pursue deeper topics, for example "Researching Blockchain applications in cross-border payments" instead of just "Building a Smart Contract to transfer money."

LEARNING PATH

6 weeks of IS355

Weeks 1-3 build foundational Blockchain knowledge; Week 4 shifts to analyzing finance and banking applications; Weeks 5-6 focus on developing and defending the project.

01 WEEK 01 Blockchain Fundamentals What is Blockchain? · Architecture · Components · How it Works
02 WEEK 02 Architecture & Consensus Block · Hash · Transaction · Consensus
03 WEEK 03 Smart Contract & DApps Smart Contract · DApp · Token · Applications
04 WEEK 04 Blockchain in Finance & Banking Analyzing Blockchain applications in finance and banking
05 WEEK 05 Project Development Project Topic · Architecture · Research · Prototype
06 WEEK 06 Project Presentation & Review Finalize · Test · Report · Defend the Project

COURSE PROJECT

From idea to product

The ultimate goal is a research project on finance/banking topics that apply Blockchain. The project workflow spans Weeks 4-6:

Choose a topic ↓ Analyze the problem ↓ Design the solution ↓ Develop a prototype ↓ Test ↓ Report & Defend

Suggested topic directions: Blockchain Payment · Cross-border Payment · KYC/Digital Identity · Trade Finance · Tokenization · Fraud Detection · Audit Trail · Supply Chain Finance.
