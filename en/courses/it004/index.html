COURSE INTRODUCTION

IT004 · Database

Cơ sở dữ liệu · Hands-on practice guide for the course

Gives students a foundation in databases and the SQL query language through a series of hands-on sessions. The practical content is organized across 6 sessions, from foundational knowledge of Databases, Tables, and integrity constraints to SQL querying, division, aggregate functions, grouping, and Triggers.

GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
